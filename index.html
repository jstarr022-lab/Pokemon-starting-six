Pokémon Starting Six Builder

Ice your starting six from the original 150 Pokémon, no legendaries: a left wing, center and right wing, two defensemen and a goalie. Tap a player to swap them out, or drag one onto another to switch spots. Then play a season with your hand-picked six, or save the image and share it.

Each round rolls a Kanto habitat and a type. Draft a matching Pokémon into any open spot, fill all six, then play an 82-game season and the playoffs. Can you win the Cup?

Season details

    Each Pokémon is graded on how well it fits its spot from its base stats, size and type. Centers want speed, playmaking (Sp. Atk) and faceoff strength (Attack); wingers want speed and scoring punch; D want Defense, HP and size; goalies want Sp. Def, Defense and reflexes, and Psychic types read the play. Ice and Water types skate a bit better, Fire types a bit worse. The goalie counts the most (30%), then the center (18%); wingers and D count 13% each, and your two weakest spots drag the whole team down. The other 31 clubs are rated 350 to 920.

    A hockey spin on the Pokémon football lineup builder idea. Sprites and data via PokéAPI.
