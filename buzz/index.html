Big screen: this device shows the board and runs the show, and everyone plays on their own phone. The game runs as long as the host's page stays open.

How to play
  • Two rounds of 6 categories with 5 clues each, then a Final Clue. Quick game skips round 2.
  • Whoever has control picks a clue. Once the clue has been read, the buzzers open. Buzz too early and you're locked out for a quarter second.
  • First buzz answers. Type your answer (or say it, where your phone supports it). Any form is fine, small typos are OK.
  • Right adds the value and gives you control. Wrong takes the value away and the others can buzz.
  • Wager clues are hidden on the board: only the player who finds one answers, and they bet first.
  • Final Clue: everyone with a positive score bets in secret, then answers in secret. The host can overrule any call.
